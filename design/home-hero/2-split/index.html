How it works

Three steps, and no account with us.

  1. 1

    Install CashPilot

    Use the desktop app on one computer. Or run the server with Docker on a home server, NAS or Raspberry Pi.

  2. 2

    Pick your services

    Choose from the catalog, sign up with the provider and paste your login. For services with a Docker image, CashPilot starts it in its own container. For the others it reads your balance.

  3. 3

    Watch every payout

    CashPilot reads your balances every hour and keeps the history. When a balance drops, it asks you whether you were paid.

What's the catch?

A fair question. Here are the facts.

  • Free

    No price, no paid plan and no ads.

  • Open source

    GPL-3.0. You can read every line on GitHub.

  • Runs on your hardware

    Your machines and your connection. We run no servers for you.

  • No account with us

    No sign-up and no email. You sign up only with the providers you choose.

How is it paid for? Some provider links are referral links. If you sign up through one, the provider may pay the CashPilot project a reward. It does not come out of your earnings. How referrals work

You stay in control

CashPilot runs on your machines. You can see what it does and stop it at any time.

  • Your logins stay on your machine

    CashPilot stores them encrypted in its own database. Each login goes only to the service that needs it.

  • Each service in its own container

    Every service CashPilot starts runs apart from the others, with its extra rights dropped and a limit on its processes.

  • Kept off your home network

    CashPilot can fence the apps it runs off your home network, so they reach the internet and nothing else. Protecting your home network.

  • Stop anything at any time

    One click stops or removes a service. A guide shows how to remove CashPilot completely.

Every provider gets a grade from A to F on the same ten questions, and each answer links to its source. See the provider grades

What changes your earnings

Nobody can promise you a figure. These are the things that move it.

  • Your country

    Providers pay more for traffic from some countries than from others.

  • Your type of IP address

    Many services pay only home connections, not servers in a data centre.

  • Your connection

    More upload speed and no data cap leave room for more traffic.

  • Uptime

    A device that stays on earns more than one that sleeps.

  • Number of devices

    Most services allow one device per IP address and a set number per account.

  • Your GPU and hardware

    GPU compute and mining depend on your card, your electricity price and the market.

Some setups earn very little. The dashboard shows what you really earn, so you can drop what does not pay.

Questions

Will it slow down my internet?

It can. Bandwidth services send other companies' traffic through your connection, so they use some of your upload and download. CashPilot shows the network use of each machine, so you can see what uses the most and stop it.

Is it legal?

It depends on where you live and on your internet contract. Some internet providers forbid sharing or reselling your connection. Check your local law and your provider's terms before you start. This is not legal advice.

Is it safe?

Bandwidth services let other people's traffic use your IP address, so read each provider's terms first. CashPilot runs each service it starts in its own container and can fence it off from your home network, so it reaches the internet and nothing else. Read Protecting your home network before you start.

How much will I earn?

We cannot tell you. It depends on your country, your type of IP address, your connection, your uptime and the services you run. Some setups earn very little. The dashboard shows what you really earn.

Why is it free?

CashPilot is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence, built by its contributors. Some provider links are referral links, and the rewards from them help fund the project. How referrals work.

Do I need Docker?

Yes, for the services CashPilot starts, because they run in containers. The desktop app works with Docker or Podman, and its setup wizard helps you install Docker. The Android app does not need Docker, but it reports to a CashPilot server or desktop app.

Does it run on a Raspberry Pi?

Yes, on a Pi 3, 4 or 5 with the 64-bit system. Most services publish a build for ARM, and CashPilot checks before a deploy whether a service runs on your CPU. Running on ARM.

Does the CashPilot project see my logins or earnings?

No. CashPilot runs on your machines and sends nothing to the project. Your logins stay in your own CashPilot, encrypted in its database. Privacy.
